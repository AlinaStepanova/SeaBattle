Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Sea Battle is a free game by Alina Stepanova. This page explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

What the app collects

Nothing. Sea Battle has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. The game runs entirely on your device, and I don't collect, store or share any personal information.

Internet access

The app uses the internet only to show this privacy policy inside the app. The page is hosted on GitHub Pages, which, like any website, receives your device's IP address and browser details when the page loads. See GitHub's Privacy Statement at docs.github.com/en/site-policy.

Google Play rating prompt

After you win a game and start a new one, the app may ask Google Play to show its rating dialog. Your rating and review go directly to Google Play and are handled under Google's Privacy Policy (policies.google.com/privacy). I only see the reviews you publish on Google Play.

Links to other apps

The menu can open Google Play (Rate, More apps), your email app (Write to author) and the Android share sheet (Share). What you do there is covered by those apps' own privacy policies.

Children

Sea Battle is suitable for all ages. It doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, email me at alina.stepanova.work@gmail.com.
